Step 1 of 5 · Recommended Time: 8 Mins

Encounter · Which Response Fits?

Read both responses in each situation. As a class, decide which response is more appropriate and explain why. Consider tone, accuracy and what the speaker can realistically promise.

1. A friend asks you to help carry a heavy box.

2. A customer asks whether a delayed package will arrive tomorrow. The carrier has not confirmed a date.

3. You need a coworker to send a file.

4. A customer asks whether they need to print a digital ticket. Printing is optional.

Step 2 of 5 · Recommended Time: 15 Mins

Clarify · Meaning, Form & Certainty

Explore how modals express different intentions in everyday and professional conversations.

1 · Meaning and use

can / can’t

ability, permission, inability

I can update the address. / I can’t access the order.

could

polite request, possible option

Could you confirm your order number?

would

polite offer, preference

Would you like a refund?

will / won’t

promise, willingness, refusal / system failure

I’ll email you. / The system won’t accept my card.

might

uncertain possibility

Your order might arrive tomorrow.

must / mustn’t

obligation / prohibition

You must verify your ID. / You mustn’t share your password.

have to / don’t have to

external requirement / lack of necessity

We have to check the policy. / You don’t have to print it.

2 · Sentence structure

Affirmative

Subject + modal + base verb

We might offer a refund.

Negative

Subject + modal + not + base verb

You must not share your PIN.

Question

Modal + subject + base verb?

Could you check the date?

Exception: Have to uses do/does in questions and negatives. Example: Do I have to pay?

3 · Important contrasts

Must vs. might

Obligation vs. possibility

Can vs. could

Direct vs. more tentative or polite request

Mustn’t vs. don’t have to

Prohibited vs. unnecessary

Will vs. might

Commitment or prediction vs. uncertainty

4 · Flip the Expression Cards

Read the function, predict a modal, then flip to reveal an example.

Step 3 of 5 · Recommended Time: 12 Mins

Remember · Modal Chomp

Use the original maze game to choose the best modal in context. Eat the correct answer and avoid the ghosts. Three lives, ten questions, ten points per correct answer.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD on a computer; swipe on a phone. If you lose all three lives, select Restart and try again. Discuss any confusing choices with your partner.

This game runs directly inside the lesson. The questions focus on B1 requests, uncertainty, promises, ability, obligation, and prohibition.

Step 4 of 5 · Recommended Time: 10 Mins

Internalize · What Would You Do?

In pairs, discuss realistic problems. Explain what you can do, what might happen, what you must do, and what you would recommend.

Missed Appointment

A client missed an important appointment and needs a new one today.

New information: There are no open slots until tomorrow.

Unavailable Product

A customer wants a product that is currently out of stock.

New information: A similar item is available but costs more.

Scheduling Conflict

Two colleagues need the same day off, but only one can be absent.

New information: One colleague has a non-refundable ticket.

Partner task: Student A suggests a solution. Student B raises a concern. Negotiate two alternatives and choose the best one. Use at least one polite request, one obligation, one uncertain possibility, and one promise or offer. Switch speakers.

Step 5 of 5 · Recommended Time: 15 Mins

Fluent Use · Problem Solving

Work in small groups. Solve both cases, agree on a realistic plan and present your resolutions to the class. Use at least five different modal forms per case.

Timing: 5 minutes per case, 5 minutes for brief presentations and feedback.
CASE 1 · Daily life

The weekend trip

Your group planned a weekend trip. One friend has a limited budget, another cannot leave before Saturday afternoon, and rain is possible. The accommodation requires confirmation today. No one has paid yet.

CASE 2 · BPO

Duplicate subscription charge

A customer reports two charges for the same subscription and needs the money back today. You can investigate and refund a verified duplicate, but must confirm the transaction date first. Processing may take three to five business days; an immediate reversal is not guaranteed.
